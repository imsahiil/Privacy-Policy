Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

Quantumleaf Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the aharasense mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App, particularly concerning personal data, health/dietary inputs, and financial transactions.

By downloading and using aharasense, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our service to you:

A. Personal Data

While using our App, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

B. Health, Diet, and Wellness Data

To provide personalized diet, nutrition tracking, and recipe recommendations, the App collects specific lifestyle and wellness metrics that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Doshas (Ayurvedic body types/constitutions)
  • Eating habits, dietary preferences, and allergies
  • Daily food logs and nutrition intake

C. Device Permissions and Data

  • Camera Access: We request access to your device’s camera. This is used solely for features within the app that require image capture (such as taking photos of your meals or updating your profile picture). Images are processed on your device or stored securely as part of your profile and are never shared with unauthorized third parties.
  • Device IDs and Usage Data: We automatically collect standard log data, such as your mobile device ID, IP address, operating system, and app performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To create and maintain your user account.
  • To authenticate logins via Email/Password, Email OTP, and Google Sign-in.
  • To personalize your nutrition tracking, recipe discovery, and diet insights.
  • To manage your premium subscriptions and access to Pro features.
  • To send transactional emails, authentication codes, and system notifications.
  • To monitor, analyze, and maintain the performance and security of the App.

3. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Our App offers subscription-based access to premium features.

  • All financial transactions and payments are handled exclusively through the Google Play Billing system.
  • We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials.
  • Google Play handles your payment data securely in accordance with their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation from Google that your subscription purchase was successful.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We securely share or process data through trusted infrastructure providers to run the App effectively. Your data may be processed by:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: For account authentication and application distribution.
  • Vercel & Hostinger: For hosting our application infrastructure and backend services.
  • Supabase: For secure cloud database storage of user profiles, nutrition logs, and app settings.
  • Resend, ZeptoMail, and Gmail: For sending transactional emails, authentication tokens (OTPs), and customer support correspondence.

We ensure that these third parties comply with industry-standard security regulations and do not use your personal information for any other purpose.

5. Data Security and Retention

The security of your data is important to us. We implement robust electronic safeguards to protect your personal and wellness data. We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide you with the App's services and to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can request the deletion of your account and associated data directly within the App settings or by contacting us at our support email below.
